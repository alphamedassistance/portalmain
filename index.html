NOTICE TO THE PUBLIC

Welcome to our Online Directory Assistance Platform.

Please be informed that all information provided within this application is online-based and is subject to change without prior notice. While we strive to keep all details accurate and up to date, users are encouraged to verify information directly with the respective medical providers or institutions.

This application is currently undergoing system updates and enhancements. While the platform remains accessible and functional, it is not yet operating at full capacity. Some features may be limited, and certain information is still in the process of being uploaded and updated.

To ensure successful scheduling, we strongly advise all users to contact the clinic or medical professional directly to confirm and secure appointments. Online requests alone do not guarantee confirmed bookings.

Kindly note that medical professionals may have existing patient queues, limited availability, and restricted schedules for online consultations. As such, delays in responses or appointment confirmations may occur.

We respectfully ask for your patience and understanding when requesting services through this platform. Furthermore, we encourage all users to maintain professionalism and respect when communicating with healthcare providers at all times.

Disclaimer

Alphamed Digital Assistance is an independent online healthcare directory and information platform. We do not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, telemedicine, or healthcare services. Information displayed on this platform is intended solely to help the public locate healthcare facilities, clinics, and licensed medical professionals. All medical decisions should be made directly with qualified healthcare providers.

Data Privacy Notice

Alphamed Digital Assistance complies with the principles of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Our platform only displays professional and directory-related information necessary to assist the public in locating healthcare providers and medical facilities. We do not collect, publish, sell, or disclose patient medical records, laboratory results, prescriptions, or other sensitive personal information.

By using this application, you acknowledge and agree to these terms. Thank you for your cooperation and understanding.
